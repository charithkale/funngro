The people behind Funngro

We built the platform we wished existed as teenagers.

Funngro is a small team on a simple mission: give India's Gen Z their first real income and real-world experience, and give companies direct access to the audience they're building for.

Mission

"Enable teens and companies to realise their full potential" — that's the whole thesis. Twenty crore teens want real work and real experience. Lakhs of companies need smart, affordable talent that already thinks like their next customer. Funngro is the bridge.
